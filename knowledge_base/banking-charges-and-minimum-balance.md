Banking Charges and Minimum Balance

## Minimum Balance Requirements

### Savings Account

* Urban branches: ₹10,000
* Semi-urban branches: ₹5,000
* Rural branches: ₹2,500

### Salary Account

* No minimum balance required

## Penalty Charges

If minimum balance is not maintained:

* Charges between ₹100–₹600 may apply depending on account type.

## SMS Alert Charges

* ₹15 per quarter for SMS alerts.

## Debit Card Annual Charges

* Standard card: ₹200/year
* Premium card: ₹500/year

## Cheque Bounce Charges

* ₹350 per bounced cheque.

## Account Closure Charges

* No charge after 12 months.
* ₹500 if closed within 12 months.
