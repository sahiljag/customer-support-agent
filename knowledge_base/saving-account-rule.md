# Savings Account Rules

## Eligibility

Any Indian resident above 18 years can open a savings account.

Minors can open accounts jointly with parents or guardians.

## Interest Rates

* Standard savings accounts: 3%–4% annual interest
* Premium accounts may provide higher rates

Interest is credited quarterly.

## Account Dormancy

Accounts with no transactions for 24 months become dormant.

Customers must complete verification to reactivate dormant accounts.

## Transaction Limits

* Unlimited online transactions
* Cash deposit limits may vary by account type

## Nominee Facility

Customers are encouraged to add nominees for easier claim processing.

## Account Statements

Customers can access:

* Monthly e-statements
* Passbook updates
* Mini statements via ATM

## Account Closure

Customers must:

* Clear pending charges
* Submit closure request
* Return unused cheque books and debit cards

Balance is transferred or paid after verification.
