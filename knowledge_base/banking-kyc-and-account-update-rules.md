

# KYC and Account Update Rules

## KYC Documents Accepted

Customers can submit any of the following:

* Aadhaar Card
* PAN Card
* Passport
* Driving License
* Voter ID

## Address Update Process

To update address:

1. Submit valid address proof
2. Fill account update form
3. Verification may take 24–48 hours

## Mobile Number Update

Customers can update mobile numbers:

* Through branch visit
* Via ATM verification
* Through internet banking (if enabled)

## PAN Card Linking

PAN linking is mandatory for:

* High-value transactions
* Fixed deposits above regulatory limits

## Re-KYC Process

Banks may periodically request re-KYC for regulatory compliance.
Failure to complete re-KYC may temporarily restrict account operations.
