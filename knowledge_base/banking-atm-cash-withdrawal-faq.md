ATM Cash Withdrawal FAQ
ATM Cash Debited but Cash Not Received

If the ATM debits money from the customer account but cash is not dispensed:

The amount is usually reversed automatically within 24 hours.
If the amount is not reversed within 7 working days, the customer should contact customer support.
Customers should provide:
Transaction ID
ATM location
Date and time of transaction
Daily ATM Withdrawal Limit
Savings Account: ₹40,000 per day
Premium Account: ₹1,00,000 per day
International ATM Withdrawal
International withdrawals are enabled only after activating international usage from mobile banking.
Additional charges may apply.
ATM Card Blocked

Cards may be blocked due to:

Incorrect PIN entered 3 times
Suspicious activity
Expired card

Customers can unblock or request a new card through support.
